How this course fits together

The course builds in three layers, mirrored by the site navigation:

  1. Foundations (Lectures 1–2): what quality is, why software is different from other industrial products, and why errors happen in the first place.
  2. Structuring quality (Lecture 3): translating quality into 11 concrete, measurable requirement factors using McCall's model.
  3. Running the system (Lectures 4–5): the organizational components of an SQA system and the engineering process — planning, executing and assessing quality activities — plus the ISO/IEC/IEEE 90003 standard that governs it formally.
